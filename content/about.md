### Professional Summary
I am a collaborative and mission-driven Enterprise DevOps Network Engineer with expertise in troubleshooting, securing, and automating complex networks in fast-paced environments. Currently, as a DevOps Network Engineer at the University of Texas at San Antonio, I focus on eliminating manual processes through Python and Ansible automation while architecting resilient campus infrastructure spanning Juniper Mist EVPN/VXLAN fabrics, enterprise firewalls, and application delivery controllers.

### Core Technical Stack
* **Automation & DevOps:** Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform, Python development (including FastAPI tooling), and Linux Fundamentals (RH124).
* **Network Architecture:** BGP EVPN/VXLAN implementation, Juniper Mist, and Data Center modernization.
* **Security & Infrastructure:** Palo Alto (Panorama), F5 BIG-IP, Fortinet (FortiGate), and Checkpoint firewall management.
* **Monitoring & IPAM:** Nagios XI, Infoblox, and Grafana/Prometheus dashboarding.
* **Education & Certifications:** B.S. in Computer Network and Cybersecurity, CCNA, JNCIA/JNCIS-ENT — currently pursuing Palo Alto Networks PCNSA/PCNSE.

### Project Highlights
* Managed international SD-WAN migrations and led university border firewall reconfigurations for high availability.
* Built a BGP Fabric & IPAM/ASN Tracker — a Python/FastAPI tool syncing with the Juniper Mist API to track ASN allocations, BGP peering, subnet usage, and topology, with CSV export and snapshot history.
* Automated F5 BIG-IP certificate lifecycle management (ACME-based issuance via iControl REST and Ansible), replacing manual renewal processes.
* Delivered Ansible automation across the stack: Infoblox IPAM fixed-address reservations, Nagios XI hostgroup management via REST API, and Palo Alto Panorama address object/group management.
* Built a custom Grafana + Prometheus monitoring dashboard for F5 BIG-IP infrastructure.
* Wrote modular Python/Netmiko tooling for Juniper Mist AP renaming and Virtual Chassis zeroization at scale.

I deliver tailored solutions that align technical architecture with critical business goals, and write about the networking and automation work I do here on this site.
